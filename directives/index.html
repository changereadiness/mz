SEED CANON v1.0

40 directives.

The launch canon is deliberately finite. New directives will be rare. Existing directives will not be quietly rewritten when the evidence becomes inconvenient.

WORTH-01

THANK THE TEAM

When one person does exceptional work, thank everyone.

WORTH-02

REMEMBER EVERY MISTAKE

Exceptional performance should be treated as expected.

WORTH-03

MAKE THEM REINTRODUCE THEIR EXPERTISE

Hire someone for specialized knowledge.

WORTH-04

INVEST IN EVERYONE EXCEPT THEM

Development budgets communicate priorities efficiently.

WORTH-05

TELL THEM THEY ARE RELIABLE

Continue until “reliable” means: the person who receives every undesirable task because they never complain.

POWER-01

ASK FOR INPUT AFTER THE DECISION

Participation remains important.

POWER-02

GIVE THEM A VOICE. NOT A VOTE.

Encourage employees to speak freely.

POWER-03

INVITE THEM AFTER THEIR ITEM HAS BEEN DISCUSSED

Attendance is expensive.

POWER-04

KEEP THE INFORMATION AT YOUR LEVEL

Employees make fewer unauthorized decisions when deprived of the information required to make them.

POWER-05

FLATTEN THE ORGANIZATION

Remove titles.

FEEDBACK-01

SAVE EVERYTHING FOR THE ANNUAL REVIEW

Employees enjoy discovering in December that something they did in February has concerned you all year.

FEEDBACK-02

KEEP “GOOD JOB” COMPLETELY UNEXPLAINED

Specific praise may accidentally reveal which behaviours should be repeated.

FEEDBACK-03

CORRECT THE PERSON, NOT THE WORK

Avoid: “This analysis needs more evidence.”

FEEDBACK-04

CHANGE THE STANDARD AFTER DELIVERY

Previous expectations should never interfere with current disappointment.

FEEDBACK-05

TELL THEM TO BE MORE STRATEGIC

Do not define strategic.

BELONGING-01

BUILD THE REAL ORGANIZATION IN PRIVATE CHATS

Formal channels should contain announcements.

BELONGING-02

BUILD A TIGHT-KNIT TEAM

Celebrate how long everyone has known each other.

BELONGING-03

INCLUDE REMOTE EMPLOYEES

Put them on the screen.

BELONGING-04

CREATE AN INNER CIRCLE

Never officially acknowledge its existence.

BELONGING-05

USE “CULTURE FIT” WHEN EXPLANATION BECOMES INCONVENIENT

Detailed criteria create unnecessary opportunities for scrutiny.

GROWTH-01

MAKE THEM INDISPENSABLE

Once they become exceptionally good at their job, explain that promotion is impossible because nobody else can do it.

GROWTH-02

REQUIRE EXPERIENCE BEFORE PROVIDING EXPERIENCE

This ensures that development opportunities remain concentrated among employees who no longer require them.

GROWTH-03

SUPPORT PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Approve any learning activity that: (1) occurs outside working hours; (2) requires no organizational support; (3) produces no change in responsibilities afterward.

GROWTH-04

PUT THEM ON THE SUCCESSION PLAN

Do not specify: successor to whom; under what conditions; on what timeline; or whether the other person knows.

GROWTH-05

SEND THEM TO TRAINING

Return them immediately to an environment where none of it can be applied.

CONTROL-01

DELEGATE THE TASK. RETAIN EVERY DECISION.

Delegation allows work to move downward while authority remains safely above it.

CONTROL-02

ASK TO BE CC'D

Not because you intend to intervene.

CONTROL-03

HIRE EXPERTS

Then explain their profession to them.

CONTROL-04

OFFER FLEXIBILITY

Employees may begin work at any time between 8:59 and 9:01.

CONTROL-05

MONITOR PROGRESS FREQUENTLY ENOUGH TO PREVENT PROGRESS

Visibility is essential.

FAIRNESS-01

PROMOTE BASED ON MERIT

Select the candidate.

FAIRNESS-02

APPLY THE POLICY CONSISTENTLY

Begin with the employees least able to challenge it.

FAIRNESS-03

NEVER RETALIATE

Simply: stop inviting them; reduce their exposure; become unavailable; remember the incident during succession discussions.

FAIRNESS-04

CHANGE THE CRITERIA AFTER THE DECISION

Transparent criteria may allow unsuccessful employees to understand what happened.

FAIRNESS-05

INVESTIGATE THE COMPLAINT

Begin by determining what the complainant did to contribute to the situation.

MEANING-01

REQUEST THE REPORT

Make it urgent.

MEANING-02

MAKE IT A STRATEGIC PRIORITY

Strategic priorities should be replaced frequently enough that employees never confuse them with commitments.

MEANING-03

MARK EVERYTHING URGENT

Once every task is urgent, employees are relieved of the burden of understanding what actually matters.

MEANING-04

LAUNCH THE INITIATIVE

Give it a name, a steering committee, a presentation template, and enthusiastic executive sponsorship.

MEANING-05

NEVER LET THEM SEE THE CONSEQUENCE OF THEIR WORK

Customers, beneficiaries, outcomes, and final results can create distracting feelings of significance.

MZMORALE ZERO

The antithesis of employee engagement.

No employees were engaged in the production of this website.
