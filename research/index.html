WORTH

Do I matter here?

Perceived organizational support; organization-based self-esteem

WORTH-001
SEED-SUPPORTED

Employees’ perceptions that the organization values their contribution and cares about their well-being are associated with important employee attitudes and outcomes.

Perceived organizational support
WORTH-002
SEED-SUPPORTED

Employees differ in the extent to which they experience themselves as valuable and competent members of their organization, and this organization-based self-evaluation relates to workplace attitudes and behaviours.

Organization-based self-esteem
WORTH-003
PROVISIONAL

Managerial and organizational behaviours that communicate investment, support, respect, or neglect may function as signals affecting perceived organizational value.

Signals of investment
POWER

Can I influence what happens here?

Psychological empowerment; employee voice; power distance

POWER-001
SEED-SUPPORTED

Greater psychological empowerment is associated with stronger job satisfaction, commitment, performance, and lower strain and turnover intentions.

Psychological empowerment
POWER-002
SEED-SUPPORTED

Perceived influence over organizational outcomes is conceptually distinct from autonomy over how one performs one’s own work.

Impact dimension of empowerment
POWER-003
PROVISIONAL

The value and future use of employee voice depend partly on whether speaking up is perceived as capable of influencing outcomes and on how authorities respond.

Employee voice / managerial receptiveness
FEEDBACK

Feedback environment; Feedback Intervention Theory

FEEDBACK-001
SEED-SUPPORTED

The quality of the ongoing workplace feedback environment is associated with supervisor relationships, performance, and employee well-being.

Feedback environment
FEEDBACK-002
SEED-SUPPORTED

Providing feedback does not inherently improve performance; effects depend on how feedback directs attention and on task/intervention characteristics.

Feedback Intervention Theory
FEEDBACK-003
SEED-SUPPORTED

Feedback tends to become less effective as attention shifts away from the task and toward self-focused processes.

Task vs self focus

SEED-SUPPORTED

Organizational identification
BELONGING-002

Workplace ostracism
BELONGING-003

Informal inclusion

GROWTH-001
SEED-SUPPORTED

Development resources
GROWTH-002

Training transfer
GROWTH-003

CONTROL

Can I decide how I do my work?

Self-determination theory; job autonomy; autonomy support

CONTROL-001
SEED-SUPPORTED

Greater perceived leader autonomy support is positively associated with autonomous motivation, need satisfaction, well-being, engagement, positive attitudes and behaviours, and negatively with distress.

Leader autonomy support
CONTROL-002
SEED-SUPPORTED

Decision-making autonomy, method autonomy, and scheduling autonomy are distinguishable and may operate through different motivational or strain pathways.

Autonomy forms
CONTROL-003
PROVISIONAL

Supervisory monitoring can function as a controlling condition when it reduces meaningful discretion or communicates that self-direction is not trusted.

Monitoring as control

Organizational justice
FAIRNESS-002

Justice consequences
FAIRNESS-003

Meaningful work

Meaning and outcomes
