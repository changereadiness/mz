THE FRAMEWORK

Eight ways to make work worse.

These are Morale Zero’s root variables: distinct territories that interact without collapsing into one generic idea called engagement.

WORTH

Do I matter here?

value, significance, recognition, respect, appreciation, investment

Make them feel insignificant.

POWER

Can I influence what happens here?

influence, voice, access, status, participation, authority, information, decision rights

Remind them where they stand.

FEEDBACK

Do I know where I stand, and can I trust what I am being told?

performance clarity, coaching, evaluation, correction, expectations, quality, timing

Keep them guessing.

BELONGING

Am I one of us?

membership, inclusion, informal access, social integration

Keep them on the outside.

GROWTH

Am I becoming more capable here?

capability, mastery, development, training transfer, career progression

Keep them exactly where they are.

CONTROL

Can I decide how I do my work?

discretion, work methods, scheduling, monitoring, delegation

Make every decision for them.

FAIRNESS

Am I being treated justly?

procedures, consistency, retaliation, selective enforcement, grievances, promotion fairness

Apply the rules selectively.

MEANING

Does what I do matter?

contribution, usefulness, impact, mission connection, task significance

Make the work feel pointless.
