COMPOUND PATTERN CANON

Damage travels in packs.

Patterns are recurring configurations of several roots. They are not ninth variables and they do not replace the Eight.

PATTERN-01COMPOUND

THE OWNERSHIP TRAP

Give them responsibility for the outcome. Keep the decisions.

Configuration: POWER down; CONTROL down; accountability up.

lack of ownership

PATTERN-02COMPOUND

THE INDISPENSABILITY TRAP

Tell them they are too valuable to lose. Use this to ensure they cannot move.

Configuration: Apparent WORTH up; GROWTH down; CONTROL down.

Over time, the employee may discover the difference between valued and useful.

PATTERN-03COMPOUND

THE INNER CIRCLE

Keep authority formally distributed. Concentrate access informally.

Configuration: BELONGING down; POWER down; FAIRNESS down.

The organizational chart remains unchanged. The actual organization does not.

PATTERN-04COMPOUND

PERFORMANCE FOG

Keep expectations vague. Make evaluation precise.

Configuration: FEEDBACK down; FAIRNESS down.

The employee learns the standard only after failing to meet it.

PATTERN-05COMPOUND

DEVELOPMENT THEATRE

Increase development activity without increasing development.

Configuration: GROWTH down; MEANING down; WORTH superficially up.

Learning portals, competency frameworks, development plans, mandatory courses — no meaningful change in capability or opportunity.

PATTERN-06COMPOUND

CEREMONIAL EMPOWERMENT

Tell them they own it. Require approval.

Configuration: POWER down; CONTROL down; empowerment rhetoric up.

Useful phrases: Take ownership. Use your judgment. You are empowered to lead. All remain subject to approval.

PATTERN-07COMPOUND

PURPOSE THEATRE

Increase discussion of purpose as the work becomes less connected to it.

Configuration: MEANING down; WORTH down; mission rhetoric up.

The organization has a purpose statement and values wall. The employee has a spreadsheet nobody reads.

PATTERN-08COMPOUND

RETALIATORY SILENCE

Ask people to speak freely. Ensure everyone remembers what happened to the previous person who did.

Configuration: POWER down; FAIRNESS down; psychological safety down.

Formal channels remain open. Usage declines mysteriously.

PATTERN-09COMPOUND

THE MOVING TARGET

Define success after observing the result.

Configuration: FEEDBACK down; FAIRNESS down; CONTROL down.

Management diagnosis: the employee needs to become more adaptable.

PATTERN-10COMPOUND

THE INVISIBLE EXPERT

Depend heavily on someone's expertise. Exclude them from the decisions where that expertise would matter.

Configuration: WORTH down; POWER down; BELONGING down.

The employee remains indispensable operationally and irrelevant strategically.
