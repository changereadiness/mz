METHOD

The joke may be merciless. The evidence must be honest.

Morale Zero keeps satire and science deliberately close—but not mixed.

THE BRIDGE

Science does not write the joke.

The science defines defensible territory. The satire exposes a recognizable organizational contradiction inside that territory. Then verification checks that the joke did not distort the mechanism.

01

STUDY → CLAIM

A paper contributes evidence to an atomic proposition. Papers are not treated as beliefs.

02

CLAIM → MECHANISM

The system asks what defensible human or organizational mechanism the evidence supports.

03

MECHANISM → CONTRADICTION

Editorial work finds observable behavior, stated intent, actual condition, and the human tension between them.

04

CONTRADICTION → SATIRE

The joke must survive as writing. The science later returns only to verify that nothing stronger was implied.

RESEARCH STANDARD

Score Claims, not papers.

Source quality and Claim confidence are different things. A prestigious synthesis can still be indirect. A single strong study can matter without becoming canon by itself. Claim language must match study design.

EDITORIAL STANDARD

Laugh first. Learn second. Reflect third.

Reality before absurdity. Clinical seriousness. Corporate language as raw material. No moralizing. No cartoon villain required.

Recognition → laughter → discomfort.

The behavior should feel observed, not merely invented.

Never explain the joke.

The research explains the mechanism. The satire is allowed to remain satire.

Zero survivors is valid.
