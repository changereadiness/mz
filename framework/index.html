THE EIGHT

The human model.

Eight root territories. Not a pre-existing validated unified scale. Morale Zero’s synthesis of established research traditions into a practical architecture for classifying morale damage.

01 / WORTH

Do I matter here?

Make them feel insignificant.
value, significance, recognition, respect, appreciation, investment

RESEARCH ANCHORS
Perceived organizational support; organization-based self-esteem

Research traditions around perceived organizational support and organization-based self-esteem examine how employees interpret signals that they are valued, respected, and worth investing in.

BOUNDARY
Distinct does not mean isolated.

Morale Zero keeps the root concept narrow enough to classify behavior, then models interaction separately rather than turning every wound into “engagement.”

SEED CLAIM LIBRARY
WORTH-001
SEED-SUPPORTED

Employees’ perceptions that the organization values their contribution and cares about their well-being are associated with important employee attitudes and outcomes.

Perceived organizational support
WORTH-002
SEED-SUPPORTED

Employees differ in the extent to which they experience themselves as valuable and competent members of their organization, and this organization-based self-evaluation relates to workplace attitudes and behaviours.

Organization-based self-esteem
WORTH-003
PROVISIONAL

Managerial and organizational behaviours that communicate investment, support, respect, or neglect may function as signals affecting perceived organizational value.

Signals of investment
